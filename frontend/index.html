How the recommendations are made

Each job is compared with your profile by the Flask backend and given a score from 0 to 100. The list is sorted by that score. Open "Why this score" on any job to see each part.

1. Skill overlap

Required skills count double, nice-to-have skills count once. This is blended with a TF-IDF cosine similarity, so rare skills count for more than common ones.

2. Experience fit

Full marks inside the job's experience range. The score drops for each year you are under or over it.

3. Location and salary

Remote jobs and jobs in your city score highest. Salary scores full when the top of the range reaches your minimum.

4. Skill gaps

Missing required skills are counted across your best matches. The ones that appear most are the quickest way to raise your scores.

match = 0.60 × skills + 0.20 × experience + 0.10 × location + 0.10 × salary
skills = 0.7 × weighted overlap + 0.3 × TF-IDF cosine

The openings are example data with made-up companies, stored in backend/data/jobs.json.
